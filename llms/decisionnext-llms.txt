# DecisionNext

> DecisionNext is an AI and machine-learning software company (founded 2015) whose
> prescriptive analytics platform helps commodity-driven businesses decide what to buy
> and sell, when, at what price, and on what formula. It serves food and agriculture
> (beef, pork, poultry, dairy, grains, edible oils) and natural resources (iron ore,
> thermal coal, LNG, copper, nickel, zinc, shipping). This file was generated by API
> Evangelist from public sources; DecisionNext does not publish an llms.txt of its own
> (https://decisionnext.com/llms.txt returns 404).

## API surface

DecisionNext publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-12 there is no developer portal,
no API reference, no SDK, no webhook catalog and no machine-readable specification
(OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection) anywhere on its public surface.
`api.decisionnext.com`, `docs.decisionnext.com` and `developer.decisionnext.com` do not
resolve in DNS. Every `/.well-known/` discovery path, plus `/openapi.json`,
`/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json` and `/api-docs`, returns HTTP 404 on `decisionnext.com`.
The product is delivered as an end-user SaaS application, sold direct and through AWS
Marketplace.

## Platform

- [Our Platform](https://decisionnext.com/products/): three layers — MarketView (commodity-specific price and supply forecasts built on USDA, CME and 100+ market sources), Enterprise (scenario, formula, mix and timing optimisation), Governance (executive benchmarking and accountability)
- Applications: MarketView, ScenarioLab, DecisionBuilder, MarketSim, DataMiner, YieldMax, MarketBench, MarketPosition, DecisionBoard
- Data management: DataFlow, ExpertSync, DataForge
- Modeling: SeriesLink, FutureSight, ModelBuilder, ModelTuner
- [Industries](https://decisionnext.com/industries/): food and agriculture, natural resources
